# Session 03) Feature Engineering
### Dataset: Smart Farming (SF24)
This dataset will be used throughout the Machine Learning module. It contains 2,200+ records with 23 features covering soil nutrients (N, P, K), environmental conditions (Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall, CO2), and farm management practices (Irrigation Frequency, Fertilizer Usage).

How we will use it:
- Regression: We will predict Water Usage Efficiency (L/kg) to optimize irrigation strategies.
- Classification: We will predict the Crop Label (22 classes) based on environmental and soil conditions.
- Clustering: We will group farms into management zones based on their resource usage.
